# Reused source

`src/lib/components/dice-renderer.ts` is adapted from the same file in the sibling Istanbul project, which in turn adapts Can’t Stop’s rounded dice geometry, ruby/ivory materials and tumble. The original projects are by Alex Nicolao and are distributed under GPL-3.0-only. The retained license text is in `licenses/istanbul-GPL-3.0.txt`.

The Nuns wrapper supplies a single die, deterministic face previews and graceful text fallback. Three.js is MIT-licensed; its package retains its license under `node_modules/three/LICENSE`.
